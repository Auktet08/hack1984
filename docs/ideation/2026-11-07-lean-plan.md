# Lean plan: what we build in the remaining hours

**Status: DRAFT, decided by North on Sat 2026-11-07 at about 14:30 EST.** This replaces the A–D experiment
shortlist (12 experiments, X1–X12). Matching edits are in `drug track/PROBLEM.md` §0, §4, §5, §10 and §13.

## The decision

**Stop chasing composite on dev. Put the remaining time into what the judges see beyond the leaderboard.**

- **The headroom is small.** E017 gives the rules every missed aspect with gold polarity and still reaches only
  0.904. Run together, the twelve research-backed experiments would add about +0.03 to +0.05 on dev. Each one is
  worth +0.005 to +0.025, below the ±0.055 dev noise, so dev can't even confirm them.
- **The judges weigh more than composite.** They also look at:
  - a network-off run on private transcripts, where rules do nothing;
  - whether the citations support the claims;
  - how the trends look in the demo;
  - usefulness to a launch team;
  - cost per 100 items;
  - the Tavily/Exa bonus and the startup.

## What we accept

- **Test will probably score below 0.843.** Levels 2 and 3 were fitted to dev, and test has more news (30 vs 5) and
  fda_label (10 vs 2) items. Without a silver hold-out we won't know by how much.
- **Mitigation:** keep the rules close to the guide's wording, and let the model fill only what the rules abstain on.
